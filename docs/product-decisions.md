# Product decisions

Owner decisions that settle open questions in the [implementation roadmap](claude-implementation-roadmap.md#17-business-decisions-to-settle-before-the-dependent-packets). They override the roadmap's recommended starting points where the two differ. Record new decisions here with their date; do not change a decision without the owner's approval.

## Record visibility and assignment (decided 2026-10-12)

| Topic                    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Staff visibility         | Staff see **only the records assigned to them**, including on their dashboard. This applies to lists, search, counts, dashboard figures, reports, exports, link pickers and recipient suggestions; hidden records must not be revealed through totals or error messages. |
| Admin and Super Admin    | An Admin sees their whole team. The Super Admin sees every team. Unchanged from the current model.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Full-team access         | Optional per-person grant, **off by default**. An Admin may give a staff member (for example a team manager) access to the whole team's records. It never extends beyond the team.                                                                                       |
| Assignment on create     | A record a staff member creates is **assigned to its creator by default**. The creator may then assign it to an approved member of the same team.                                                                                                                        |
| Reassignment consequence | A staff member without full-team access who reassigns a record loses sight of it. The interface must say so before the change is confirmed.                                                                                                                              |

### How assignment works (decided 2026-10-12)

| Topic                       | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is assigned            | **Clients (including Leads) and Properties** carry the assignee. Activity follows its parent: Follow-ups, Viewings, Shortlist, Interaction log and Client care are visible to whoever holds the linked Client; owner Contacts are visible to whoever holds a linked Property. |
| Direct task assignment      | A **Follow-up may also be assigned directly** to a teammate (for example "call this landlord") without handing over the whole client. Staff see the follow-ups assigned to them plus those of their clients.                                                                  |
| Existing records            | When assignment is introduced, each existing record starts with **its creator from the activity history** if that creator is an active staff member of the team; otherwise it goes to the Admin. Nobody loses their current work on release day.                              |
| Approved teammate           | An **active** member of the same team who holds the module permission for that record (for example scheduling for Follow-ups). Only the current assignee, the Admin or a full-team member may reassign.                                                                       |
| Departures                  | When staff are suspended or archived, their records move to the **Admin queue** ("Unassigned · needs owner"). The Admin can reassign in bulk. Assignment history is retained.                                                                                                 |
| Record IDs                  | The **server issues new record IDs** for staff, so ID suggestions and "already in use" errors never reveal records the person cannot see.                                                                                                                                     |
| Hand-off                    | Keep the loss-of-visibility warning, add an optional **hand-off note** shown to the new assignee, and an option to keep the sender informed once notifications exist (P2).                                                                                                    |
| Dashboards and filters      | Staff dashboards count only their own records. Admins and full-team members get an **"Assigned to"** filter for any team member's workload.                                                                                                                                   |
| Full-team access boundaries | Full-team access never overrides module permissions; a member without finance permission still cannot see finance records.                                                                                                                                                    |

### Assignment details (decided 2026-10-12, after security review)

| Topic                               | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contacts                            | Owner/landlord **Contacts also carry an assignee**, so a contact a staff member creates stays visible to them even before it is linked to a property.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Client on a directly assigned task  | A staff member given a Follow-up for a client they do not hold sees that client's **name, phone and email only**, never the rest of the profile. This narrow exception applies only to Follow-ups directly assigned to them.                                                                                            |
| Record IDs                          | **Sequential workbook-style IDs are kept** (for example CL-045). Staff may infer approximate team volume; this is accepted for Excel compatibility.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Deleting parent records             | Until granular actions (roadmap A2) exist, **only Admins and full-team members may delete Clients, Properties and Contacts**. Staff can still edit records they hold and delete their own activity records.                                                                                                             |
| Viewings on a held listing          | The holder of a Property sees that other agents' viewings are booked on it, showing **date/time and the responsible agent only**, with no client details. The same limited entries also appear in the holder's **Dashboard upcoming viewings** (decided 2026-10-15): not clickable, no client name, status or feedback. |
| Passing on a directly assigned task | A staff member given a client's Follow-up directly **may complete it or return it to the client's holder, but not pass it to a third person**. Only the client's holder, the Admin or a full-team member can re-hand it, so the client's contact details stay under the holder's control. (Decided 2026-10-12.)         |

## Action permissions (decided 2026-10-13)

Settles the owner questions in the [Action permissions design](action-permissions-design.md) (roadmap A2).

| Topic                                     | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Per-action grants                         | For each connected module an Admin ticks what each staff member may do: **Add, Edit, Hand on, Delete activity** and **Delete clients & contacts / Delete properties**. Every current staff member keeps exactly today's abilities on release day; new sensitive grants start off. Admins and the Super Admin keep full access. |
| Deleting clients, contacts and properties | A **separate tick, off by default**, available only with whole-team visibility. Current whole-team members keep it. Assigned-only staff never get it.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Deleting needs the linked modules         | A member may delete (or change the ID of) a client only if they can also see Scheduling and Properties; a property only with Scheduling; a contact only with Properties. This keeps hidden linked records from being revealed by a delete error.                                                                               |
| Hand on                                   | Whole-team members with **Hand on** may move anyone's records, including bulk moves and the Admin queue (12 Oct rule). An Admin makes a view-only supervisor by unticking it.                                                                                                                                                  |
| View-only members                         | A member who can only view a module **can still be assigned records** in it; they see them but cannot change them.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Directly assigned follow-ups              | A staff member given a client's Follow-up directly may complete or return it but **not delete it**. Only the client's holder, the Admin or a whole-team member may delete it.                                                                                                                                                  |

## Finance: deals, payments and expenses (decided 2026-10-14)

| Topic                  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Commission splits      | Configurable at three levels: a **team default** company/agent split set by the Admin; a per-agent **agreement** that overrides it, with a start date so new agreements never change older deals; and **per-deal** second-agent splits. Only Admins override a deal's split, with a reason.                                              |
| Split snapshot         | When a deal is earned, its split is **saved as it stands**; later agreement changes never rewrite past commissions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Calculation order      | Fee (agreed value × rate, or fixed fee) → external partner/co-broker share first → remainder split between company and agents. Commissions are calculated before VAT.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Second agent           | Whether a second agent's share comes from the first agent's share or the company's share is a **team setting**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| VAT                    | If the Admin marks the company **VAT-registered**, 5% VAT is calculated and shown separately; otherwise no VAT.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Visibility             | Admins see all team finance. Agents see **only their own** deals, payments and a personal commission statement — never company totals or colleagues' earnings.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Deal ownership         | A deal is **assigned to an agent** like clients, with the same visibility rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Payments and expenses  | Agents **record and submit**; they show as Pending approval. The Admin (or a person the Admin grants approval rights, e.g. an accountant) **approves** — only approved records count and they are locked except for Admins. **Rejected** records stay, marked Rejected with a reason, never count, and can be corrected and resubmitted. |
| Currency and precision | **AED only** for now (other currencies later). Amounts are stored exactly to the fils.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| History                | Every financial change is recorded in history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

## Cross-company deal agreements (decided 2026-10-15, planned)

Many agent-to-agent and broker-to-broker deals in the UAE rest on verbal promises, and disputes have no evidence. Blaqks One will act as the neutral record keeper: deal hand-overs, co-broking and commission shares between companies (and between agents of different companies) become recorded, provable agreements. This extends the in-company hand-over with a referral share. It is planned after the core CRM and needs architecture and security review before implementation.

| Topic                      | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who may accept             | Only the **named recipient** an agreement was sent to may accept it. Agents may accept cross-company deals sent to them without Admin approval.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Agents outside Blaqks One  | **May accept.** The agreement is shared as a link (copied, or sent by WhatsApp or email). A member who opens it signs in, accepts, and it is filed in their account with a notification indicator. A non-member who accepts is invited to create an account; declining sign-up is fine and the acceptance still stands.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Recipient check (proposed) | The link works only for the named recipient: members must be signed in as that person; non-members confirm a one-time code sent to the named phone or email.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Licence                    | **Optional**, because not every agent has a RERA BRN. Recorded as licence number plus issuing authority (RERA, ADREC or another emirate). The certificate shows the verification level: Basic (phone verified, always required), Standard (plus verified email and company), Licensed (plus licence), Strong (UAE PASS, later). The sender may set a minimum level per agreement.                                                                                                                    |
| Unlicensed warning         | Shown to the sender before sending, worded so it does not scare users away: "This agent has no licence on record, so this agreement may not hold as strong evidence. Adding their licence number makes it stronger." A UAE lawyer reviews the wording before launch.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Evidence                   | Accepted agreements are locked and tamper-evident; changes are amendments both sides accept, keeping every version. Both sides (including guests) keep a copy and a downloadable certificate. Blaqks One records and tracks payments due but never holds or moves money.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Who may send               | **Every active member** may send an agreement. It is not a permission: Admins cannot switch it on or off for staff (decided 2026-10-20).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Design defaults            | Confirmed 2026-10-20 (agreements design §11): the three types `handover_full`, `handover_referral` and `co_broking`; the referral share is capped by the team's referral setting; offers expire after 14 days by default and 90 at most; agreements are kept five years after completion or cancellation; only the minimal property and client snapshot is shared, client contact off by default; accepted agreements change only by a new, re-accepted version; the Super Admin sees metadata only. |
| Proposed improvements      | Decline or counter-offer with every round recorded; expiring, revocable links; no amounts in chat link previews; minimal sharing (no client details unless added); earlier agreements attach to a guest's account when they later sign up with the same verified contact. Awaiting owner confirmation.                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Messaging design (decided 2026-10-20)

The owner approved the [messaging design](messaging-design.md) (packet X0) after its [security review](messaging-security.md), taking the recommended answers:

- **Key custody:** an environment key for local development only; a managed secret store or KMS before production.
- **Retention:** messages and conversations 24 months after last activity (then text erased), notes 24 months, opt-out evidence 5 years, provider receipts and delivery jobs 30 days, change history 5 years; to be confirmed against UAE data protection advice before launch.
- **Social replies:** a separate Facebook/Instagram reply permission, off by default.
- **Activity:** channel and Inbox events appear in Team activity, redacted.
- **Media:** shown as "Media received" until private media storage exists.
- **Phone region:** UAE for every company for now; a per-company setting when a company outside the UAE needs it.
- **Inbox design:** the 8-screen CRM Inbox design canvas is approved as the build reference for the Inbox, Channels and lead-rules screens, with TikTok as its own Channels card.
- **Email system (D4):** revised by the owner on 2026-10-21: **Twilio SendGrid first** (packet E3). Each member gets a sending address on the company domain at no per-person cost, and email is written and read only in the CRM (on desktop or the phone browser), with no separate Gmail or Outlook mailbox. The Google Workspace adapter built in E1 (2026-10-20) stays available for companies that pay for Workspace. Both need the company's own domain, which the owner has not bought yet. E3 details (owner, 2026-10-21, [SendGrid design](sendgrid-design.md)): each company connects its own SendGrid account; Keys with Simoni's domain is used only by the CRM; a company uses SendGrid or Google Workspace for client email, one at a time; a departed member's address is reserved and its mail goes to Unassigned; an address given by mistake may be removed while it has never been used with clients (owner, 2026-10-21). Archiving an Admin disconnects all of the company's channels in the same transaction, deleting their keys and releasing the domain, number or Page; conversations and history stay for the Super Admin (owner, 2026-10-21).
- **TikTok:** added as a fifth channel on the Channels screen (owner, 2026-10-20). Real TikTok messages come in packet T1, after TikTok approves Business Messaging access and UAE eligibility is confirmed.
- **Account ownership:** real providers prove which number, mailbox or Page a credential controls; Admins never type it. Each member connects their own mailbox, with Admin approval.
- **Team chat groups and mentions (owner, 2026-10-22):** any member may start a private group with colleagues of the same company (up to 21 people); only its participants can read it — the Admin cannot open a group they are not in and the Super Admin reads no company chat. People are added without having to accept and may leave; any participant may add colleagues later, and someone who left may be added back. People added later see the whole history of the group. Mentions notify inside the CRM only, never by email or push. See [Team chat](team-chat.md).
- **Moving old property photos into storage (owner, 2026-10-22):** the trusted backfill command writes no Team activity entry, as an exception to the activity rule: activity must name a real person and this is a one-off system task; stored files and record versions trace it. See [Media storage](media-storage.md).
